Attractions

Freddy and the Gang

Our main stage show featuring Freddy Fazbear and friends. Performances every hour.

Kid’s Cove

A hands‑on “take apart and put together” experience inspired by classic animatronics. Guests can help assemble (and disassemble) our friendly character, Mangle.

FazCade

Retro and modern arcade games, high scores, and a few machines that occasionally turn on by themselves.

FunZone Boat Ride

A gentle indoor boat ride through themed scenes. Please keep hands inside the boat at all times — especially near animatronic maintenance hatches.
